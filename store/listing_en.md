## Long Description (max 4000 chars)

```
MazePrint turns your Android phone into a maze factory.

Generate a brand-new maze every time, solve it with your finger, then print it as a PDF to enjoy on paper — alone, with friends, or with the whole family.

────────────────────────────
🔲 SQUARE & ⬡ HEXAGONAL MAZES
────────────────────────────
Two maze types to keep things fresh:
• Square mazes — classic rectangular grids, great for beginners and experts alike
• Hexagonal mazes — pointy-top hex cells for a unique spatial challenge

────────────────────────────
🎮 SMOOTH GAMEPLAY
────────────────────────────
• Drag your finger in any direction to move the player
• Tap a reachable cell to jump straight to it
• Trail shows where you've been — fades over time to keep the challenge alive
• Camera follows you with smart edge clamping
• Overview button for a bird's-eye view
• Timer and move counter

────────────────────────────
🏆 4 DIFFICULTY LEVELS
────────────────────────────
• Easy — small maze, long corridors (DFS algorithm)
• Medium — balanced, more junctions (Prim's algorithm)
• Hard — large maze, many decision points
• Extreme — massive grid for the most dedicated solvers

────────────────────────────
📄 PRINT-READY PDF
────────────────────────────
Create a printable PDF in seconds:
• 1 to 5 mazes per PDF — same level or a difficulty progression
• Auto-optimized layout: 4 mazes per page for Easy/Medium, 1 per page for Hard/Extreme
• Custom title for your printout
• Seed number on every maze (so you can reproduce it)
• Designed for A4 paper
• Share or print directly from your phone

────────────────────────────
🌙 DARK THEME · 🌍 6 LANGUAGES
────────────────────────────
Elegant dark interface, easy on the eyes.
Available in: English, Italian, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German.

────────────────────────────
🔒 PRIVACY
────────────────────────────
No account required. Works 100% offline. Settings and scores are stored only on your device.

MazePrint is free to play and supported by non-intrusive ads.
```
