What you get

Everything a maze lover needs

⬜

Square Mazes

Classic rectangular grids with long corridors (DFS) or many forks (Prim's algorithm). Four sizes from Easy to Extreme.

⬡

Hexagonal Mazes

Unique hexagonal cells for a fresh challenge. Perfect pointy-top orientation with smooth drag and tap-to-move controls.

🎮

Intuitive Controls

Drag your finger or tap the destination. A smart camera follows your progress, and a trail shows where you've been.

🏆

4 Difficulty Levels

Easy to Extreme — each with proportions designed to fit a phone screen perfectly and print beautifully on A4.

🌙

Dark Theme

Elegant dark interface that's easy on the eyes. Optimized for AMOLED screens.

🌍

6 Languages

Available in Italian, English, Spanish, Portuguese, French, and German.

Print feature

From screen to paper in seconds

Generate a PDF with 1 to 5 mazes — same difficulty or a progression — and print it straight from your phone. Perfect for classrooms, family game nights, or waiting rooms.

Each maze includes a custom title, the seed number for reproducibility, and layouts optimized for A4 paper.
