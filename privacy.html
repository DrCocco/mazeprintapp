Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 27, 2026  ·  Effective: May 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how MazePrint ("we", "our", or "the app") handles information when you use our Android application. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

1. Data we collect

MazePrint is designed to work entirely offline and does not require registration or an account. We do not collect your name, email address, or any personally identifiable information directly.

Data stored locally on your device (never transmitted to our servers):

  • Selected language preference
  • App settings (e.g., persistent trail toggle)
  • Number of mazes completed (displayed as a local counter)

This data is stored using Android's SharedPreferences and remains on your device. It is deleted when you uninstall the app.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

MazePrint uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and process information including:

  • Advertising ID (a resettable device identifier)
  • Device type, operating system version, and language
  • IP address (used to determine approximate location)
  • Interaction data with advertisements (views, clicks)

This data is collected and processed by Google LLC under its own Privacy Policy. We do not receive or store this data on our servers. You can opt out of personalized ads at any time in your device settings: Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization.

For more information, see: Google Privacy Policy and AdMob data practices.

3. Data we do NOT collect

  • We do not collect your name, email, or contact information
  • We do not collect precise GPS location
  • We do not access your camera, microphone, or contacts
  • We do not share your data with third parties (except as described in Section 2 for AdMob)
  • We do not sell your data

4. Children's privacy

MazePrint is suitable for all ages, including children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The advertising shown may be limited or disabled for child-directed content in accordance with COPPA and Google Play policies.

5. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when new features are added (e.g., online leaderboards or accounts in a future version). When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

6. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:
